How to assess the options

Before choosing, write down the pages and features you need. Then ask each provider to explain the proposed scope, what they need from you and how decisions or revisions will be handled. Key points to compare include:

  • Project timing: this varies with the number of pages, features, content readiness and review process. Ask for stages and an estimate based on your actual brief rather than assuming every site follows the same schedule.
  • Cost considerations: the scope, design needs, content support, requested features and ongoing services can affect the cost. Ask what is included and which items would be separate.
  • Ownership and upkeep: clarify how you will access and update the website, and who is responsible for hosting and maintenance.
  • Useful terms: WordPress is a platform for building and managing websites. Hosting is the service that makes a website available online. SEO, or search engine optimisation, refers to work that helps search engines understand website content.

Look at examples from a provider and consider whether the information is easy to follow. Ask how the design will work on phones, how the site can be maintained and what happens if your requirements change. A direct answer makes it easier to compare the service with your needs.
