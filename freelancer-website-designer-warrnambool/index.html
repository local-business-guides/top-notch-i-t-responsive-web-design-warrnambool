Scope, timing and ways to compare providers

A website project usually starts with a conversation about your business, audience, content and goals. From there, agree on the pages and features, who will supply text and images, how feedback will be handled, and what support is available after the site is published.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: New website design, WordPress, redesign, hosting, maintenance, SEO and ongoing support are services Top Notch I.T offers.
- Typical timing: The schedule depends on the size of the site, how ready your content is and how quickly decisions and feedback are provided. Ask for expected stages and dates before work begins.
