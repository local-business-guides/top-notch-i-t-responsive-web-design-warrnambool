What the work can include, and what to check

Top Notch I.T offers new website design, WordPress websites, redesigns, hosting, maintenance, SEO and ongoing support. The right combination depends on what you need. Here are a few key facts to keep in mind:

  • Scope: A project may involve a new site or changes to an existing one, along with hosting or ongoing care.
